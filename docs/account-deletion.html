Account & Data Deletion

For the GreenThumb — Plant Care application by Ton Tsukanov. Updated: 2026-10-28

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data from the GreenThumb — Plant Care mobile application (the "Application"), and how long copies remain afterwards.

How to delete your account — in the Application

You can delete your account directly inside the Application. No email is required. (Labels in the Russian version of the Application are shown in brackets.)

  1. Open the Application on your device and sign in with your recovery key.
  2. Go to the Profile tab [Профиль], the right-most tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Tap Delete Account [Удалить аккаунт], below the Sign Out [Выйти] option.
  4. Read the Delete account [Удаление аккаунта] confirmation. It lists what will be deleted.
  5. Tap Delete my account [Удалить аккаунт] to confirm. The deletion cannot be undone. Afterwards your recovery key no longer works and cannot be used to sign in again.

If you cannot open the Application

If you cannot open the Application or have lost your recovery key, request deletion by email:

  1. If you still have your recovery key (for example, saved in Google Password Manager or another password manager), copy it.
  2. Send an email to greenthumb.taunt861@passmail.net with:
    • Subject: Account deletion request
    • Body: paste your recovery key
  3. You will receive a confirmation reply within a reasonable timeframe (typically a few business days). The account is removed from the live systems within 14 days of the confirmation; backup copies then follow the retention periods below.

If you cannot access your recovery key, email the same address and describe what you remember (approximate date of first use, plant names, device details). The developer will try to locate and delete your data on a best-effort basis.

What data will be deleted

  • Your user record (recovery key, reminder time, language, seasonal mode and time zone) — after which the recovery key stops working
  • All plants you created (names, species, care schedules, care dates and notes)
  • All cover photos and timeline photos with their captions; the photo files are removed from photo storage
  • Your care journal (watering, fertilizing, repotting and pruning events, and journal notes)
  • Your achievements and streak data
  • All your push notification subscriptions (push tokens)
  • All your sign-in sessions on every device

On the device where you delete the account, the Application also removes the stored recovery key, the cached account details and the local copy of your plants.

When the data disappears

  • Live systems: the data leaves the live database and photo storage immediately when you confirm the deletion. A deleted photo can still be served from the server's short-term photo cache for up to 1 day.
  • Hourly database backups: backups made before the deletion still contain your data. They are replaced as backups rotate, so your data is gone from hourly backups within 48 hours.
  • Daily database backups: gone within 14 days.
  • Pre-upgrade snapshots: before a database upgrade, the server keeps one extra snapshot. At most 10 such snapshots exist. A snapshot made before your deletion stays until newer snapshots replace it, which depends on how often the server is upgraded.

Backups are kept in private storage and are used only to restore the service after a failure.

What data may be retained

  • Plant diagnosis requests: if you used plant diagnosis, OpenAI may keep those requests (photo and plant context) for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, and then deletes them. They do not contain your recovery key or account number.
  • Server logs: web server access logs (IP address, time, requested address) are deleted after about 15 days; technical error logs after about 8 weeks.
  • Usage counters: the daily counts of identification and diagnosis requests are kept as anonymous totals that are not linked to any account, so daily limits stay correct.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are not linked to your account and stay under Firebase's retention rules.
  • Google Password Manager: if you saved your recovery key there, the entry stays in your Google account until you remove it. The key no longer works after deletion.
  • Email: correspondence for an email-based deletion request may be kept by your email provider and by the developer's email provider under their own retention policies. Deletions done inside the Application do not create any correspondence.
